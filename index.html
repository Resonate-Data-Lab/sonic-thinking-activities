              About

              What this is

              Sonic Activities is a collection of short things you can do with sound — listening exercises, recording prompts, warm-ups, sketching and sound-design tasks.

              Some are quiet and solitary. Some need a room full of people. Most take under twenty minutes, and none of them need special equipment beyond a phone.

              How to use it

              If you don't know where to start, press Give me a random activity. If you know what you're after, browse and filter by how long you have, what the activity is about, and whether you're on your own or with other people.

              Every activity has

              • How long it takes and whether it's solo, paired or for a group
              • A one-line overview and short instructions — not a long script
              • Questions to think about afterwards
              • Examples of what it can look like, and a box to add your own

              It's meant to grow

              Responses and suggestions both go straight into the collection's own database, so the site fills up as people use it.
